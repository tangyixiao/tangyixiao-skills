## Relationship between sources

academic-humanizer credits blader/humanizer for its general pattern catalog and mentions koaeraser/ARMS as an influence. Both supplied license notices for academic-humanizer and blader/humanizer are retained here. This project does not vendor ARMS code or claim its authorship.

agent-toolkit contains many development and professional skills. Only its humanizer and writing-clearly-and-concisely entrypoints informed this writing skill. Its historical reference reproductions are not bundled here.

## Integration changes

- One shared workflow replaces repeated and conflicting pipelines.
- Factual and technical fidelity takes precedence over stylistic rules.
- Evidence, author voice, meaningful negation, operational conditions and adverse results remain explicit.
- Default output is the requested final artifact; review-only and local-edit scope remain distinct.
- References and illustrative fixtures are newly organized; example details never become facts in user text.
